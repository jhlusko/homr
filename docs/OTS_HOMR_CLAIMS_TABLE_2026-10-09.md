# OTS-HOMR review-assisted lyrics and staff text: claims table (refreshed 2026-10-10)

A draft for the owner's review (refreshed 2026-10-10 for provider v11: no beams, no doubled ties, pinned-core figures, evaluation-set exposure), not an approval to publish and not a statement about the hosted service (OTS-HOMR is not
live in production; production is about 200 commits behind). Every number says what was counted, who judged it, and
whether it is **held-out** or **development** evidence. Extends `LYRIC_RELEASE_READINESS_2026-10-08.md`, whose "do not
advertise" list still applies.

**Evidence classes.** `H` = scored on pages no earlier step had touched, rules fixed beforehand. `D` = development: the
rule, threshold or idea was designed or tuned while looking at (some of) these pages, so the figure is an upper bound.
`O` = owner-judged. `A` = scored automatically against the native OpenScore source (a scan's printed marks can differ
from the encoded score, which understates availability). "Unsure" always counts as wrong.

**Exposure.** Rows that depend on the recognition core's reading of a page (4, 5, 7, 8, 14) are only unbiased on songs the core never trained on. Checked against the core's training indexes on 2026-10-10: the owner-judged direction confirmation set (row 5) is clean (its 27 songs are in neither the training nor the validation index); the 10-page lyric-placement set (row 4) is not (8 of 10 songs have systems in the core's training indexes); the 30-page set scored automatically on 2026-10-10 (rows 5 and 7) is not (27 of 30). The core's validation split (13 Lieder and 9 quartet scores) is the clean pool used from row 13 on.

## B. Claims not to make

| Do not say | Why |
|---|---|
| "Reads all lyrics" / any recall or 100% recognition figure for lyrics. | The 95% conditional lyric-retention measurement (readiness doc) is still unclosed; counts of suggestions are not recall. |
| "Automatically places lyrics, directions or dynamics." | Placement is a proposal; roughly one in ten (lyrics) to one in eight (directions and dynamics) is wrong, and every one needs a reviewer. |
| "Reads dynamics" without qualification. | OCR reads p and pp well and f, ff, mf poorly; the classifier adds a few f marks (precision 7 of 9, recall low); p, pp, ppp are deliberately left to OCR. Hairpins are not read at all. |
| "Finds pedal markings, tempo words in any language." | Vocabulary is Italian, German and French terms listed in code; "Ped." depends on OCR; German tempo phrases were missed. |
| Any lyric or direction figure for instrumental, orchestral or non-Lieder scores. | Lyric, direction and dynamic evidence is Lieder (voice and piano); instrumental pages were used for latency and negative controls only. Recognition accuracy is separate (rows 12-14). |
| "Handles every page" / failure-free. | About 5% of audited pages are not scoreable (cover, text-only, white-on-black notation: 4 of 83); two inverted pages need an offline fix that is not shipped. |
| Anything that treats the development figures (parts of 2-5) as untouched test results, or quotes 87% placement as held-out. | Rules and filters were fitted after seeing judgements; a confirming fresh set exists only for rows 5 and part of 2. |
| "No false lyric suggestions on instrumental scores" as an absolute. | Measured below (row 11): 3 false suggestions on 2 of 41 lyric-free pages, so the supportable statement is a rate with its sample, not zero. |
| "Reads beams", or any claim that its beaming beats the editor's default. | Beams are no longer emitted. On 1,108 beamable notes of 27 pages, scored against the printed beaming (owner-verified), HOMR with the editor's default beaming matched on 63.7% and OTS-HOMR's own beams on 56.1%. |
| "OTS-HOMR is better on songs" / "on all music". | On songs with piano the two engines are level on 23 of 25 lines (row 14); the advantage is on ensemble music, and PDMX windows are rendered crops. |
| Any example, number or comparison drawn from songs the core was trained on, presented as unseen. | 199 of 215 Lieder score directories are in the core's training indexes; only the 13 validation songs and 9 validation quartets are clean. The first engine-comparison gallery made this mistake and was replaced. |
| "In production", "available", "live". | OTS-HOMR is not deployed to production. |

## C. Draft public copy (built only from section A)

> **Lyric and staff text review (preview).** After a scan, review the lyrics, written directions (cresc., rit., dolce...)
> and dynamic marks (p, mf...) found on the page, beside the original. Correct the text, choose the note each one goes
> on, and add what you accept to your score: nothing is added until you accept it. Suggestions can miss marks, misread
> text, or confuse printed instructions with sung words, and about one placement in ten is wrong, so your review matters.
> Marks the scanner cannot attach to a note are still offered; you choose where they go. About half of the printed
> directions and dynamics on a page are found; you add the rest. Tested on vocal scores with piano (OpenScore Lieder); other
> kinds of music have not been evaluated for this feature.
