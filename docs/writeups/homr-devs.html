    Structured heads

    Frozen-core projections predict beam levels, hooks, ties, advance delta, stem direction, slur event/side, and dynamics. The original GrandStaff-only sidecar validates beams, ties, and advance but has no directional-stem, phrase-slur, or dynamic labels. A mixed GrandStaff/Lieder/PDMX frozen-head run has real held-out support: ties macro-F1 .844 (16,693), up/down stem F1 .811 (217,631), slur span F1 .772 (3,400), slur-side macro-F1 .723 (3,121), and nontrivial advance macro-F1 .751 (166,624). Dynamics macro-F1 is .105 (935) and is withheld.

    Stage 3: page text (supplementary)

    A separate detector supplies regions; lyrics use OCR/sequence reading and dynamics use a closed marking classifier plus attachment. On 307 held-out pages: Lyrics 66.7% precision, 94.1% recall, 78.1% F1 (3,555 boxes); Dynamic 75.9/93.9/84.0 (429); measure numbers 75.5/94.9/84.1 (78). Tempo, staff text, expression, and fingering are not ready for always-on output. Scan supervision changes lyric patch IoU .581 synthetic-only→.966.

    Data and hard lessons

    The original scan/reference join used page/system indices across different editions. Rebuilding from whole-score MusicXML with independent geometry and reverse-fingerprint alignment reduced >50-point collapses from 56.7% to 7.9% in a 900-staff audit, raised scanned mean accuracy 46.8→89.9, and reduced median drop 76.7→0.0. Clefs and barlines/repeats were restored; ambiguous one-to-many lines, shifts, and truncation were reviewed instead of counted as recognition errors. Full dataset methods.

    1. Do not add synthetic replay to a scan-adapted model by default.
    2. Do not trust patch IoU as page quality.
    3. Do not trust unit tests to prove export.
    4. Invalidate duration caches after tuplet rewrites.
    5. Do not report rare-class macro scores without support.
