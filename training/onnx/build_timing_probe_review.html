Timing-head learning

6 October 2026 · frozen September core

Four independent heads learn elapsed duration, written duration, tuplet ratio and full-measure rests. OSSQ and Lieder contribute equally to the training objective. Pitch and rhythm core weights stay frozen.

This is a teacher-forced experiment. Preceding tokens come from ground truth. These curves test what the frozen representation can learn; they do not establish free-decoding improvements. OSSQ training is one score and validation is only eight crops from two scores.
