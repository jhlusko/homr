## What moved, and what it is worth

**The metre numerator was missing from three converters.** `music_xml_parser` emitted
`timeSignatureBeats_N`; `humdrum_kern_parser`, `primus_semantic_parser` and
`music21_kern_parser` did not. The last is a *scoring* backend and `validation/smb.py`
defaults to it, so that benchmark was charging the tool for a token its reference could
not produce. All three fixed; grandstaff reconverted (15 staves gained, 0 lost).

**An OSSQ "regression" was a stale reference set.** `v4_s42` first measured -0.37pp on
OSSQ. Its references predated the numerator (0 of 3,912 carried one), so a *correct*
insertion shifted whole staves to near zero. Regenerated as `phase7num`; the sign flips
to +1.71pp. `phase7num` is also the first OSSQ build carrying repeats at all (124 of
3,912; `phase7fix` and `phase7clef` carry none).

**The v4 corpus effect, vocabulary-controlled** (against `nat42`/`nat7`, which share
v4's vocabulary but not its corpus): PDMX +1.4 to +1.6pp, OSSQ +1.1 to +2.6pp, **Lieder
not significant in either direction**. The +8.15pp Lieder headline against `426` was a
vocabulary gap, not the corpus.

**OSSQ was excluded from training for a reason fixed three builds ago.**
`train_lieder_only.py` cites 56.7% mislabeling; RUNLOG 10718 measures the repair at
56.7% -> 7.9% collapsed. Putting OSSQ back is what `scans_v4` is.

## Next steps — two arms, one variable each

Four variables differ between `v4_s42` and `scans_v4` (OSSQ added, replay composition,
replay fraction 26%->14.9%, epochs 12->6), so neither the +1.93 nor the -0.98 is
attributed. Both arms below hold epochs at 6 and differ from `scans_v4` in exactly one
thing.

```bash

#   scans_v4 - A = what grandstaff replay bought.        ~75 min

  nohup .venv/bin/python -u -m training.transformer.train_scans \

# ARM B - the OSSQ contribution. Varied replay, no OSSQ.

cd /workspace/b0/homr && HOMR_RUN_SUFFIX=armB OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=8 OMP_NUM_THREADS=8 \

Score each with `/workspace/b0/score_scansv4.sh` (edit `CK` and `OUT`), then:

python -m training.transformer.compare_checkpoints --name OSSQ \

  --run "armA=.../armA_scores/ossq.jsonl"
```

**Two caveats to state before running.**

The seed noise floor is ~0.15pp (`v4_s42` and `v4_s7` sit 0.02pp apart on OSSQ). One run
per arm resolves OSSQ's +1.93 cleanly but may not resolve the -0.48/-0.98 losses.
Attributing those needs two seeds per arm.

Arm B cannot hold the replay *fraction* fixed while removing OSSQ: the same 6,700 replay
files are 15% of `scans_v4`'s mixture and 65% of B's. That is unavoidable - dropping
34,510 files changes the ratio by construction - so B measures "OSSQ plus the ratio change
it forces", not OSSQ alone. If the Lieder loss is dilution, B is where it comes back, and
that is the reading to take from it.

## Operational traps that cost time today

- **Cap BLAS threads before any `multiprocessing.Pool`.** Uncapped workers each spawn a
  ~128-thread pool; three grandstaff reconversions hung at ~53,790 of 53,882 files with
  the pool idle and never printed `Done indexing`, while a concurrent scoring run died
  with `pthread_create failed`. Use
  `OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 MKL_NUM_THREADS=1` and a pool of ~16.
- **Never read a count from a job that has not printed its completion marker.** Those
  hung runs left plausible-looking partial `index_tmp.txt` files; comparing two of them
  produced a wholly fictitious "+51 files" result.
- **`pgrep -f <script>` over ssh matches the ssh wrapper running the check.** It reports
  a finished job as running. Kill by PID.
- **rsync from this box needs `--partial --append-verify` and a retry loop.** The link
  resets often; a plain `rsync -a` failed and the resumable version succeeded on attempt
  2 for the same transfer.
- **`HOMR_RUN_SUFFIX` is mandatory for a replicate.** Without it two runs from the same
  checkout share a run id, and `train_transformer` returns early when the output file
  exists - a "retrain" that finishes in seconds having trained nothing.

## Review tooling

`docs/writeups/review.html`, served from `docs/writeups/` (`python3 -m http.server 8899`).
Sets are registered in `SETS` and the nav builds from it - do not add buttons by hand.
Two new sets: `?set=checkpointV4` (426 vs v4) and `?set=checkpointScansV4` (v4 vs
scans_v4). Exports land as `omr-review-<set>.json`;
`training/omr_datasets/summarise_checkpoint_review.py` splits a review into reading
difference, vocabulary, and reference defect, which is the only question the aggregate
cannot answer.

**What the two reviews established.** In the 426 comparison, 48% of judged movement sat
on staves called "no real difference" and 13% on a wrong reference - only 39% was better
reading. In the `scans_v4` comparison, with vocabulary held constant, "no real
difference" fell from 22 of 61 to **1 of 50**, and of ten staves the metric scored worse,
seven were judged better and three were label defects - **zero genuine regressions**.
The per-staff metric disagrees with a musician most sharply where the label is wrong.
